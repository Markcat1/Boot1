# SafeZMG & Resilience Core - Go-to-Market Strategy

## Executive Summary
This document outlines the strategy to acquire the first 50 corporate clients (B2B) in the industrial/commercial sector in the Guadalajara Metropolitan Area (ZMG) while scaling the social impact (B2C) of the platform.

## B2B Acquisition Strategy (The First 50 Clients)
1. **Target Audience:** Industrial parks (e.g., El Salto), large commercial plazas, and corporate office towers that face strict municipal civil protection regulations.
2. **Value Proposition (The "Hook"):** Emphasize risk mitigation and *compliance automation*. SafeZMG prevents fines for expired dictates and organizes complex PIPC (Internal Civil Protection Programs) documentation.
3. **Direct Sales & Partnerships:**
   - Partner with established Third-Party Accredited Consultants (Terceros Acreditados). Offer them the platform to manage their portfolio of clients more efficiently. If a consultant brings 5 clients, that accelerates the path to 50.
   - Direct outreach to Facility Managers and EHS (Environment, Health, and Safety) Directors.
4. **Pilot Program:** Offer a 30-day "Audit Ready" trial where the team helps digitize their current PIPC. The tangible result is a dashboard showing exactly where they are non-compliant.

## B2C Social Scaling Strategy
1. **Freemium Community Model:** The core alert system and shelter map remain completely free for citizens.
2. **Local Government Synergy:** Present the platform to municipal civil protection units (Zapopan, Guadalajara, Tlajomulco) as a free citizen empowerment tool. Their endorsement provides instant credibility and user acquisition.
3. **Hyper-local Outreach:** Focus initial marketing on known vulnerable settlements (e.g., areas prone to flooding near Arroyo Seco). Use SMS campaigns or community leaders to onboard users to the low-data mobile web app.

## Monetization Model
- **B2C:** Free (Freemium).
- **B2B Tiered SaaS:**
  - *PyME:* Basic document tracking.
  - *Industrial:* Multi-facility management, brigade training tracking.
  - *Corporativo:* API access, advanced risk analytics, priority consultant matching.
- **Marketplace:** Take a % fee for connecting businesses needing immediate on-site audits with certified local consultants.
